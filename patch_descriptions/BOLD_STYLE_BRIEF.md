# Bold patch style brief

The operator reviewed real Nano Banana output and rejected it. The current prompts
produce technically clean but **generic, cartoonish icons**: one big simplified
silhouette, a couple of flat background shapes, almost no internal information.

## The target

What the operator liked (the Skyfowl they kept): a muscular golden-brown forearm and
talons gripping a bundle of black arrows, with **layered flames** (deep red outer /
orange mid / bright yellow core), red diamond embers, black arrow fletching, thick
confident black outlines, real anatomical construction, filling the square on a
strong diagonal. It reads like a **bold vintage tattoo-flash / screen-print poster
illustration**, not a flat icon.

## The failure mode

What the operator disliked: a big black bomb circle with a squiggle fuse, a flat
cream highlight and a simple multi-lobed fireball — cute and toy-like. And a first
Skyfowl that was a featureless brown blob holding simple arrows.

## Five levers — use all of them

1. **Bigger dynamic composition** — the subject fills or nearly breaks the frame on
   a strong diagonal; no dead space; foreground/background layering.
2. **Layered colour depth** — give major forms 2–3 **flat** tone steps (base + a
   darker shade band + a lighter highlight), e.g. a flame = deep red outer / orange
   mid / yellow core. Separate solid shapes only — **never** gradients or soft
   shading.
3. **Thicker, bolder outlines** — one heavy, confident near-black keyline around
   every major shape.
4. **Less cute / more aggressive** — real construction and weight; gritty and
   serious, not a rounded toy. Add real structural elements as BIG bold shapes
   (e.g. a metal casing band and rivets on a bomb; feather/fur planes; anatomical
   segmentation).
5. **Push this patch individually** — no need to match the other patches; make this
   one as bold as its subject allows.

## Hard embroidery constraints (keep)

- **Up to 6 flat colours.**
- Flat solid fills only: no gradients, no soft shading, no texture, no halftone.
- **No small or fine details** — the operator explicitly does not want detail that
  is hard to embroider. Every added element (rivets, feathers, talons, embers) must
  be a **large bold shape**, not fine linework. Any internal detail line must be at
  least ~1.5 mm thick at the patch's size.
- **Bold pointed tips are allowed** on large shapes (flame licks, arrowheads,
  talons, horns) but must stay chunky — no hairline or needle points, nothing
  tapering below ~1.5 mm.
- High contrast, clean hard boundaries, readable at the stated size.
- Plain white background with a **clear white margin reaching all four corners**;
  square composition; no text/lettering; no drawn border ring (the pipeline adds
  the satin border).

## Keep

- The canon subject and signature palette from the existing file.
- The patch shape and size.
- The `## Prompt` / `## Follow-up edits` / `## Spec` structure, and the **Pipeline**
  line in the Spec (update `--colors N` if the thread count changed).

## Deliverable

Overwrite the same prompt file with the revised version. Keep the prompt under
~220 words. Add follow-up edits guarding the new failure modes: design drifting back
to a flat centred icon; gradients/shading creeping in; details becoming small and
fine; losing the layered tones.
