# Dungeon Crawler Carl — Anarchist's Battle Rattle Patch Layout

> Note: There is no canonical patch map. Dinniman almost never states where a patch goes, and his one left/right answer is contradictory. The fandom wiki uses the *viewer's* POV; cosplayers were told to use the *wearer's* POV. Everything below is best-plausible, drawn from cover art + fan consensus, not canon. Pick the wearer's POV and stay consistent.
>

## Notes on the description column

- **Two patches are explicitly "back" patches in canon**: the Scavenger's Daughter is described as a *back upgrade patch*, and the nine-spinning-skull patch sits on the back. If you're placing both, decide which owns the center-back and push the other to a lower back panel — the books don't resolve this.
- **Size cue for scaling**: Earth = "tiny/half-dollar"; Peace is *the same size* as Earth; Midnight Epicure and Book Burner are both "medium-sized"; Skyfowl is small/square, ~3 in on merch. Use these to keep relative scale right.
- **Only one patch has an in-text placement**: Katia sews the Skyfowl/arrow patch *next to the Earth patch on the shoulder*. That's the single most solid anchor you have — build the rest around it.
- Everything else remains fan inference, and the author's left/right answers are inconsistent, so pick the wearer's POV and stay consistent.

## Source-quality caveats

- The Fandom wiki pages for **Bomb**, **Skyfowl**, and **Reaper Spider** were Cloudflare-blocked; their descriptions come from the system-codex mirror, the book text, and merch listings, so those three are thinner than the others.
- The canonical wiki patch list is **10 entries**. Some fan names are nicknames: "Spider/Eye" = Reaper Spider Minion; "Lucky Bastard" = Four-Leaf Clover; "Goat Head" = Midnight Epicure; "Arrow" = Skyfowl.
- The list keeps growing — book 8 (May 2026) added material, so a few patches have no location chatter yet.
