**Shape:** die-cut spider silhouette (a fan convention; `patches.md` gives no die-cut instruction for this patch and flags its wiki source as thin — overrides the default circle/square/shield list) · **Size:** 70 x 70 mm · **Threads:** 6 · **Palette:** #1A1A1A - near-black (keyline, pupil, bead eyes, strap, deep shadow), #566070 - deep slate grey (abdomen shade band, far legs, far basket), #8A93A0 - mid grey (second grey tone step), #C8CDD4 - silver grey (head, near basket, highlights, eye glint), #B8720F - deep amber (jewel-eye outer ring), #F2B33D - bright amber (jewel-eye core)

## Spec

- **Subject:** Traveling Reaper Spider Minion merchant (Midnight Market trader) — a small "black and silver", die-cut, Shi Maria-like spider carrying multiple wicker baskets lashed over her bulbous body. (Die-cut shape is a fan convention; `patches.md` flags this patch's wiki source as thin.)
- **Composition:** Shape unchanged — still the pure die-cut spider silhouette (no background disc added, so the canon die-cut read survives; depth comes from layering flat slate and grey shapes instead). Bold graphic-poster read, low three-quarter mid-stride: the huge bulbous abdomen lunges lower-left to upper-right on a strong diagonal, nearly filling the square with a clear white margin reaching all four corners; eight chunky jointed paddle legs, four far legs in deep slate grey tucked behind the body; the abdomen and head carry three flat grey tone steps (deep slate shade band, mid grey, silver highlight); head turned to the viewer with one huge layered amber jewel eye (deep amber outer ring, bright amber core, chunky silver highlight, solid black pupil) and two black bead eyes; two big silver baskets riding the diagonal under one thick black strap, near basket overlapping the body in front. Heavy and grim, not cute.
- **Finished size:** 70 x 70 mm — canon "Upgrade Patch (Small)"; one step above the 60 mm tiny Earth/Peace rounds so thick legs and baskets still stitch cleanly, well below the medium patches.
- **Embroidery notes:** 6 threads; near-black carries the satin keyline, pupil, bead eyes and strap; deep slate grey is the darkest flat tone (abdomen shade band, far legs, far basket); mid grey is the second grey tone step; silver grey makes the biggest fills (head, near basket) plus the highlights and eye glint; deep amber and bright amber layer the jewel eye. Every tone is a separate flat fill — no gradients or soft shading. Legs are held to ≥6 mm wide with ≥6 mm gaps so each leg's inscribed radius clears the 2.5 mm border carve and can carry its own satin edge; any internal line stays ≥1.5 mm. White background unstitched, and the pipeline traces the dynamic spider silhouette for the satin border.
- **Pipeline:** run `tools/digitize/run.sh --key-background --colors 6 --size-mm 70 --config patch_descriptions/border_black.yaml <image>`. The raster path only detects a satin border on transparent input (hence `--key-background`); `--border-color` is SVG-only and `run.sh` drops it for raster inputs, and the auto border colour is sampled from the outer band — the field colour on light patches, i.e. invisible. The shared config forces `border.mode: force_black` so the satin border is always a visible dark line.
