## Spec

- **Subject:** An eagle forearm and talons clutching black arrows whose tips burn — the twelve flaming arrows of the Skyfowl hero Radiant Star. The dozen is deliberately merged into one bundle (see below).
- **Composition:** A thick golden-brown forearm thrusts in from the lower-left corner with real muscle construction (one broad warm-sand highlight plane, one heavy division line); three bold talon toes grip one thick near-black arrow bundle sweeping diagonally corner to corner to the upper right; behind the arrowheads the upper right is one huge layered flame mass — deep red outer, orange mid, bright yellow core — in three big flame licks; three chunky near-black arrowheads overlap the flames in front with a yellow lick at each tip; near-black fletching blocks crop the lower-left; big deep-red diamond embers fly toward the top-left; one heavy near-black keyline outlines every major shape — bold tattoo-flash energy filling the square.
- **Finished size:** 75 x 75 mm - canon calls it a small square patch and merch runs ~3 in (76 mm), so it sits between tiny Earth (60 x 60) and the medium patches (100 x 100).
- **Embroidery notes:** 6 threads; the flame is the set's deepest layering — three solid tones (deep red outer, orange mid, golden-yellow core) instead of one flat orange, and the deep red doubles as the ember diamonds, which is the new sixth thread; the forearm and talons get a second tone from the warm-sand highlight plane, reusing the field thread. The twelve arrows are deliberately merged into one thick bundle — a defended departure from canon's "a dozen", because twelve separate shafts would produce twelve sub-1 mm slivers at 75 mm; the fanned tips stay distinct as blunt notches ≥8 mm. Every feature is a big bold shape (talon toes, arrowheads, fletching blocks and embers ≥8 mm; internal lines ≥2 mm), and the field sits inside a ≥10 mm plain-white margin so the corner-block background detection keys cleanly; the near-black keyline does double duty as the arrow colour and the set's satin linework.
- **Pipeline:** run `tools/digitize/run.sh --key-background --colors 6 --size-mm 75 --config patch_descriptions/border_black.yaml <image>`. The raster path only detects a satin border on transparent input (hence `--key-background`); `--border-color` is SVG-only and `run.sh` drops it for raster inputs, and the auto border colour is sampled from the outer band — the field colour on light patches, i.e. invisible. The shared config forces `border.mode: force_black` so the satin border is always a visible dark line.
